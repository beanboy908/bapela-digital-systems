Common Questions

We follow a 50/50 model. A 50% commencement fee secures your project on our dev calendar, and the balance is paid once you are satisfied.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

Your enquiry opens a WhatsApp conversation where we can discuss your business, what you need customers to do, and the best starting package before a quote is prepared.

Can you build custom features?

Absolutely. As software developers, we code custom logic for orders, bookings, and complex data tracking.
