Frequently asked questions

Before you
book.

Where is Blink & Blush? +

Appointments take place in Solomon Mahlangu Annex. Theo will confirm the exact meet-up details when she replies on WhatsApp.

Do I need to bring anything? +

Only bring your own lashes if you choose the BYO option. If you’re not sure how to apply them, tick the help box — Theo can guide you.

How do I know my booking is confirmed? +

Your WhatsApp message is a booking request. Theo will reply to confirm availability and your appointment time.

Can I book for a friend too? +

Absolutely. Mention how many people you’re booking for in your WhatsApp chat and Theo will help coordinate it.
